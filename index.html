How to interact — point + pinch, nothing else

  • Point your index finger to move the cursor. Near-misses snap to nearby bodies.
  • Pinch (thumb + index together) to grab the highlighted body. The ring cinches as the pinch closes — it grabs at full close.
  • Move while pinching to drag it, release to drop. Thresholds self-calibrate to your hand; watch d= in the debug box.
  • Two-hand pinch: move apart/together to zoom, twist to rotate.
  • No camera? Use the mouse: move to point, hold left button to grab.

Camera (mouse / keys)

  • Drag empty space to orbit · wheel to zoom toward the cursor · right-drag to pan.
  • Arrows orbit · +/- zoom · R reset view · X reset world · T top · F edge · V overview · O auto-orbit · H this help.
  • 1–7 switch presets (Space / Blocks / Test / Black hole / Drive / Atom / Voxel).
  • High quality adds procedural planet textures + bloom glow.

Drive preset

  • Steer with pointer left/right · pinch-hold = gas (pinch gently for partial throttle) · release to coast · Space = brake.
  • Easy mode (button or E): point at the ground, pinch to drop a pin, pinch again to GO, pinch once more to stop.
  • Stay inside the neon rails — the grass slows you down. Hard cornering and braking puff tire smoke. Cross the start line for lap times.

Atom preset

  • Grab an electron and drop it on another shell (n=1, 2, 3).
  • Dropping to a lower shell emits a photon flash labeled with its true wavelength (3→2 glows red at 656 nm).

Voxel preset

  • Tap a pinch to place the selected block on the targeted face.
  • Hold the pinch to break the block (highlight reddens) — sliding off cancels. Bedrock never breaks.
  • Pick blocks from the palette below, or press Q/E to cycle.
